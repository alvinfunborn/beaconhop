# BeaconHop Tabs privacy notice

BeaconHop Tabs connects your browser to the BeaconHop application on your computer so you can find and switch tabs with the keyboard.

The extension reads tab titles, URLs, window and tab identifiers, and browser-cached website icons. It sends this information to your locally installed BeaconHop application using the browser's Native Messaging interface. It does not send this information to the extension developer or an analytics service, and contains no advertising or remote executable code.

Page previews are optional and disabled until you grant the requested permission. When enabled, the extension captures small images of pages you visit in the currently visible tab. Previews stay in memory on your computer, with limits on image size, count and capture frequency. They are removed when the extension stops, when you disable previews, or when their tabs close or navigate. Preview capture excludes private browsing tabs. BeaconHop may hold its own in-memory preview cache while its switching panel is in use.

You can disable and clear previews from the extension popup, disable the extension in your browser, or uninstall it at any time. Disabling a plugin in BeaconHop stops BeaconHop from using it; use the browser's extension controls to stop the extension itself.

The installed desktop application and browser are required for this local integration. This notice describes the BeaconHop Tabs extension; websites you visit and other installed software have their own privacy practices.
